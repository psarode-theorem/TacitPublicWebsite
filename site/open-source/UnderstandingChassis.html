Understanding Tacit-Chassis / Open Source

Philosophy: The Open Source Chassis will provide the core capabilities and base automation to orchestrate the Discovery to Outcome workflow needed to realize a base Agentic Mission Room, which Providers can fork and implement. Industry forks customize it by implementing or integrating third-party adapters to new tools.

Voice interviews, diagrams, e-signature, risk engines: each plugs into a capability port, so a fork gets the tool without giving up the rules.

How a fork comes together

Three decisions, no rewrite.

01

Start from an industry fork

Take the Open Source Chassis and add your playbook, stages, roles and record fields. Or start from one of ours.

02

Integrate your tools

For each core capability, choose or build the adapter that does the job: voice, diagrams, e-signature, scoring, your model with your own key. Swap later without touching the mission.

03

Deploy your way

Self-host it in your data center or your client's, or let us run it as Managed Hosting. The same code runs both ways.

Mission workflow

Draft. Confirm. Commit.

Every capability and every adapter feeds the same three steps. Agents draft, a named person confirms, and each party keeps what is theirs.

Step 1

Draft

Agents and adapters propose: interview transcripts, records, scores, diagrams. Every output is marked pending and carries its source.

Step 2 · the Lead

Confirm

The named Lead alone decides what becomes a record. No agent or adapter can confirm, set a threshold or close a mission.

Step 3

Commit

The mission closes with a full audit trail. Documents and signatures act only on what the Lead confirmed.

Why ports

Governance travels with every adapter.

Swap, don't rebuild

A mission does not know which vendor served it. Change the adapter and the records and audit trail stay the same.

Every adapter keeps the rules

It declares who it is and where it processes. The tenant's Lead approves it. Its output is a draft. Calls are logged by size, never content. It fails closed, and ships with a fake for local runs.

A route for vendors and builders

A good adapter reaches every fork that needs the capability. It enters the base through a specified change, never a side door.
