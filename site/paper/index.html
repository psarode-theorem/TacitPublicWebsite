Tacit Work Needs a Mission Room

Accountability, Ownership and Data Sovereignty in Agent-Assisted Professional Services
P. Sarode · R. Nair · Adjunct Researchers-in-Residence, Future of Ways of Working, Theoremlabs.io

Expertise engagements between providers and their clients depend on knowledge that has never been written down. They run on tools built for conversation and file sharing, which record neither who knows what, who owns what, where it resides, nor who decided. AI agents can now elicit, draft and score at scale, which makes those gaps more consequential. This paper describes the Mission Room: a workspace organized around a bounded mission, in which the client's context remains the client's and stays where the parties agree, the provider's expertise remains the provider's, agents draft, and a named person confirms.
