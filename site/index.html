Give judgment work a workspace of its own.

A bounded workspace for expert engagements between a provider and its client. Agents draft. One named person stands behind every finding.

The problem
Nobody can say who decided.

What matters in an engagement was never written down. Weeks later no one can say which interview a conclusion came from, who confirmed it, or where the client's material sits. Agents draft in minutes, so the gap grows.

Our hypothesis
A room, not a channel.

One client, one mission, a start and an end. Inside, agents draft and a named Lead confirms; the client's context and the provider's method stay on separate shelves. We hypothesise both sides will trust it more than chat and file shares.

From paper to open source. The Mission Room ideas in the paper are being built as open-source software under Apache-2.0, free to fork for your own kind of work. The paper sets out seven hypotheses we want to test with partners. We are looking for academic feedback, industry collaboration and design partners.

What working with us looks like

One real mission, one client. Not a pilot of software; a test of the idea.

1. ScopeOne engagement you would run anyway. We map it to a Base playbook with you.
2. RunA Lead (you) and an Operator run the mission; agents interview and draft.About 4 to 6 weeks.
3. ConfirmYou confirm every record. The client sees the committed result, read-only.
4. PublishWe write a case study together, using consented, aggregated data only.

Free for the first design partners. We will not name you or your client without written permission. Your data and IP stay yours (below).

Who owns what

Stays with you

  • Your data and your clients' content: interviews, documents, transcripts, records.
  • Your intellectual property, including your Base playbook, which is your method. Client details are never written into it.
  • Your export and deletion rights, per tenant (in development).

Stays with Theoremlabs.io

  • The software IP of Tacit Chassis. It is open source under Apache-2.0: anyone may use and fork it, and Theoremlabs.io remains the owner and steward.
  • The Mission Forks that Theoremlabs.io builds, with their playbooks, templates and data models.

What we may do with design-partner data: only what you consent to, in aggregate, for research. Never model training. Never named without written permission. The optional control plane sees metadata only, never mission content.
